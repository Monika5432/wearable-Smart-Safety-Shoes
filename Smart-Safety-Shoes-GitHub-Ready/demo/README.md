## Recommended demonstration flow

1. Introduce the Smart Safety Shoes and the problem in one sentence.
2. Show the electronics up close.
3. Trigger the emergency mechanism.
4. Show the ESP32 response (serial/status output).
5. Show location acquisition.
6. Show the emergency alert arriving on the contact's phone.
7. Show the final shoe prototype being worn.
8. Close with limitations and what's next — judges respond well to honesty about what isn't finished yet.

**Tip:** Keep the video under 3 minutes. Lead with the trigger-to-alert moment — it's the single clip that proves the concept works.
